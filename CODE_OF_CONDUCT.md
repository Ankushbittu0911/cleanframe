# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming and respectful community. Participants agree to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and considerate of differing viewpoints. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and abusive behaviour are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code and may take appropriate action in response to repeated or severe violations.

For concerns, contact the project maintainers through the repository's issue or contact channels.
